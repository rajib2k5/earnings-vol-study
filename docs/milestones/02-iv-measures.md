## 1. What we built and why

Milestone 1 said which announcements the study has and on which session each reached the market. Milestone 2 measures
**what the options priced around them**, from the DoltHub bid/ask quotes alone.

1. **The atom, `chain_expiry`** (local, 181,597 rows). For each company, session and expiry: the put-call-parity
   forward, the ATM total implied variance w by Black-76 on the out-of-the-money side, interpolated in log-moneyness,
   the sessions to expiry τ and the session-annualised IV $\sqrt{252\,w/\tau}$ (ADR 0003 §0).
2. **`iv_daily`** (committed, 85,650 rows). Per company and session: the front ATM IV, the **event-aware 30-day IV**
   `cm30_iv` and its event-blind twin, the event variance **E**, the implied moves and the diffusive IV (ADR 0003 §a–§c).
3. **`event_iv`**, a new table (committed, 1,353 rows). Per announcement: E and the implied move on day −1, the 30-day
   and front-expiry crushes, the change in ordinary volatility, the realised day-0 move from the parity forwards and
   the label-free move `abs_move_cand_max`. They are the IV columns of the planned `event_measures`, built early as
   `session_snapshot` was at milestone 1; milestone 3 takes them over.
4. **The funnel, completed.** A new step drops events without a 30-day IV on day −1 or day 0, and the baseline counts
   only sessions with one (ADR 0004 §1): the primary sample is **347 events**, a measured count, where milestone 1 had
   the upper bound 350. `by_move_tercile` (ADR 0002 §3 job 3) and `evar_nonpositive` are filled.
5. **Contract 0.3.0 and an "IV measures" page** in the app: histograms and quantiles of the implied move √E and of the
   30-day crush over the primary sample. Python bins them; the app only draws bars.
6. **Validation**: our ATM IV against the vendor's `vol` column (prediction P6), the leak of the event-blind 30-day IV,
   and ADR 0003's AAPL oracle reproduced through the pipeline's own code path.
7. **The notebook** `notebooks/02_iv_measures.ipynb`, generated by `tools/build_notebooks.py` and checked by
   `tests/test_notebooks_sync.py`.

The data decisions changed during the milestone (owner, via the coordinator, 2026-10-29). The final rule: real data
the app and docs need may be committed, no bulk raw files, files under about 10 MB. What that means here is in §6.4.

## 2. Why this design

| Choice | Alternatives | Why | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| The atom as a **pure function on arrays** (`chain.expiry_atom`), the grouping in `measures/run.py` | pandas per expiry (milestone 1's `parity_forward`) | 181,597 expiries; the array version runs the whole history in about 50 s. The pandas `parity_forward` stays as a wrapper of the same core (`forward.forward_from_pairs`) | Two entry points to one forward |
| **Brent only where a root exists**: `has_root` checks every strike at once, and only the bracket strikes are solved | Solve every strike | The bracket needs 2 solves; validity needs only 2 prices per strike | A strike's w is solved lazily (`w_at`), so `n_strikes_valid` counts strikes with a root, not solved ones |
| Forward ties in \|C − P\| ranked in **whole cents** (2\|C − P\| as an integer) | The float \|C − P\| | Quotes are cents, so a decimal tie is exact; a float broke 109 of them the wrong way (review claim 2a) | 89 of 180,055 forwards changed (at most 0.43%), and their IVs by up to 2.99 vol points |
| Quadratic fallback **only for a too-wide bracket**, with a valid strike on each side of k = 0 in its window, drawn around **w_ref** = the bracket's linear w; weights 1/relative spread | Fit on one side of the money (no bracket), with w_ref = the nearest strike's w (the build's rule); linear across the wide bracket | A one-sided fit extrapolates: on 177,388 real hold-out expiries it missed the known IV by p95 1.53, max 41.6 vol points; linear across a too-wide bracket has the quadratic's p95 (1.16 against 1.15) and twice its bias (ADR 0003 §0 step 4, revised after the review, claim 3b) | 127 of 181,597 expiries use the quadratic; the 166 one-sided fits are now `no_atm`, and one primary event (RTX 2025-04-22) lost its day-0 30-day IV |
| **Spans** when `as_of(expiration)` ≥ the first session on or after the announcement date D: day 0, or **D itself for an AMC release** (or no label) | expiration ≥ day 0, which classed an expiry on an AMC day clean | Its holders decide on exercise until 5:30 p.m. ET, after the release (FINRA Rule 2360), so it is classed spanning, conservatively (ADR 0003 §b, revised after the review, claim 7). `as_of` itself changes no class (the review's Channel A, E3c); τ uses the same `as_of` | 157 `chain_expiry` rows moved from clean to spanning: 31 for 7 AMC events, 126 for 16 unlabelled ones |
| The next event is the first **effective day 0** after the session; duplicate rows have none | Calendar dates | ADR 0003 classifies by day 0; data model §3.5 gives an unplaced event next(D) | A wrong label moves the class of a few days around an event |
| `sigma_d_iv` from **E's pair**, else the two shortest clean expiries | Always the clean pair | It is the forward variance of the same model E comes from | Two definitions to explain; the fallback is pinned by a test since the review (claim 8) |
| E from the two shortest **usable** spanning expiries: a spanning expiry without an ATM w is skipped | No E that day | Under the model any two spanning expiries give the same E; only the noise changes (ADR 0003 §c, "Which expiries") | 235 of the 19,601 E rows use a T2 beyond a skipped spanning expiry |
| `event_iv` written by **measures**, not panel | `event_measures` at milestone 3 | ADR 0001: a column is written by the first layer that has all its inputs (iv_daily, chain_expiry, events); the funnel needs it now | A table milestone 3 folds away (R24) |
| **Pre / post** = day −1 / day 0, else day −2 / day +1 (one session at most), for every sample | Daily events at −1 / 0 only | ADR 0004 §4's rule; the funnel's `crush_observed` step still requires −1 and 0 in the primary sample | `crush_span_sessions` must be read with the crush |
| **T1\*** = the shortest `ok` expiry at pre with τ ≥ 5 at day 0; at post interpolated in τ, **never extrapolated** | Any listed expiry; extrapolation | ADR 0003 §a; extrapolating a front crush would invent the number we measure | 83 of the 347 primary events have no front-fixed crush |
| Realised move on the shortest expiry with a forward on both days and τ ≥ 5 at post, carry at the **start day's** rate | The front expiry of each day | The same expiry on both days is what removes the carry (ADR 0003 §e) | – |
| `abs_move_cand_max` needs **both** legs | Use whichever leg exists | One missing leg could hide the news move and drop a mislabelled event into the low tercile, which job 3 exists to prevent | 285 of 1,353 events lack it: 228 in `coarse` (in 88 no expiry has a forward on both ends; in the rest an end has no snapshot within one session), 36 in `none` (28 dated after the data end), 14 `daily`, 7 `mixed` (one of them the `duplicate_row` BRK.B:2024-11-01, which has no move by design) |
| Funnel: **`iv_usable` before `baseline_minimum`**, and the baseline needs a `cm30_iv` per session | Fold into one step | Each rule is visible with its own count | One more step for the app |
| Terciles **by rank** (ties by `event_id`), `share_gap_contradicts` among events **with a verdict** | Quantile cut points; all events | Deterministic and equal-sized; a missing verdict is not a non-contradiction | – |
| `iv_measures.json`: **histograms and quantiles**, `sqrt_mean_evar` over every E, signed | Per-event points | When the view was designed, the app JSON had to be aggregate (coordinator's message of 2026-10-29, later lifted). E ≤ 0 events are counted, never dropped | A per-event scatter waits for `implied_vs_realised` (milestone 4) |
| **`iv_daily` committed** (4.7 MB), `chain_expiry` local (17.0 MB) | Both local; both committed | The data model lists `iv_daily` as committed; it is under the 10 MB per-file limit. `chain_expiry` is above it. (*Corrected after the review:* this gave "it holds the FRED discount factor" as a reason; `DTB3` may be committed, and `iv_daily` uses the same discount factor) | 4.7 MB of binary in git; a rebuild that changes it rewrites it |
| `timing_check.gap_verdict_yf` committed through **`Table.keep_restricted`** | Commit all of `timing_check`; leave the share null | The owner allowed real values the app needs; the funnel needs the verdict only. The writer still drops every other class-R column | A Yahoo-derived label in `data/derived/` under Yahoo's terms, not CC BY-SA. *After the review (claim 15a)* every notice says so: `data/derived/LICENSE` and `README.md`, the root `DATA.md`, `app/DATA.md` and `meta.json` (the About page) |
| `vendor_iv_current` left **null** | Extract `volatility_history` from the mirror | ADR 0003 ranks it the third validation check; the vendor `vol` of `option_chain` (already extracted) answers P6 | One column empty until a later milestone |
| ADR 0003's constants in **`params.yaml`** (`iv` section) | Keep them in code | ADR 0001: one parameter file, in the hash | The params hash changed, so the stage was rebuilt in a new folder (the committed milestone-1 tables came out byte-identical) |

## 4. Data flow

```
 cache/dolt/options@<commit>/option_chain/<yyyy-mm>.parquet   (bid, ask, vol per strike; local)
        │ measures/run.py snapshot_quotes: kept snapshot of each session (session_snapshot), calls | puts
        ▼
 chain.expiry_atom ── forward (4 strikes) ── OTM side, bounds, |k| ≤ 0.5 ── Brent w ── ATM in k ── τ, sqrt(252 w/τ)
        │                              ▲ DF from market_daily.r_cont (FRED DTB3)
        ▼
 chain_expiry  (cache/stage/<hash>/, local, 181,597 rows)
        │ tables.iv_daily_row: ok rows with τ ≥ 5, split clean | spanning by the next event (events; tables.spans)
        ▼
 iv_daily  (stage + data/derived, 85,650 rows): front, cm30 (event-aware), cm30_blind, E, sigma_d, implied moves
        │ event_iv.event_row: pre/post, T1*, realised move (chain_expiry forwards), abs_move_cand_max
        ▼
 event_iv  (stage + data/derived, 1,353 rows)
        │ tools/export_app_data.py (committed tables only: events, eps, session, iv_daily, event_iv,
        │                           timing_check.gap_verdict_yf)
        ▼
 app/public/data/sample_funnel.json (6 steps, terciles, E ≤ 0)   iv_measures.json (histograms)   meta.json (M2)
```
